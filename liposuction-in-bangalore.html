Overview

What is liposuction?

Liposuction is a body-contouring procedure that removes stubborn pockets of fat through tiny incisions using a thin tube called a cannula. It targets areas that don't respond to diet and exercise, such as the belly, love handles, thighs, upper arms, chin and male chest (gynaecomastia).

Liposuction is not a weight-loss treatment or a cure for obesity. It works best for people who are close to their ideal weight, have good skin elasticity and want a slimmer, more proportionate shape. The fat cells removed don't come back, so results last as long as you keep a stable weight.

At Veinex Health we perform liposuction in Bangalore with a strong focus on safety. We use tumescent and laser-assisted techniques that reduce bleeding and bruising and help the skin tighten. Every patient gets an honest consultation about what liposuction can and can't achieve.

Who is a good candidate for liposuction?

Within about 10–15 kg of your ideal body weight
Stubborn fat pockets that resist diet and exercise
Firm, elastic skin
Good general health, with no uncontrolled medical conditions
Non-smoker, or willing to stop before and after surgery
Realistic expectations about the results

Liposuction is not a substitute for weight loss. If you have significant obesity or loose, sagging skin, your surgeon may suggest weight management first or a different procedure, such as abdominoplasty.

Treatment

Liposuction techniques at Veinex Health, Bangalore

Your surgeon chooses the technique based on the area, the amount of fat and your skin quality.

Tumescent liposuction

The area is filled with a numbing solution that shrinks blood vessels, so fat can be removed with less bleeding and bruising.

Laser-assisted liposuction

Laser energy melts fat before removal and helps the skin contract, which is useful for the chin, arms and small areas.

Power-assisted liposuction

A vibrating cannula removes larger volumes of fat more smoothly and precisely.

Gynaecomastia correction

Liposuction, with gland removal if needed, to flatten and reshape the male chest.
