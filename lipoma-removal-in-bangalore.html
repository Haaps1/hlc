Overview

What is a lipoma?

A lipoma is a soft, slow-growing lump of fat that sits just under the skin. It feels doughy, moves slightly when you press it and is usually painless. Lipomas are common on the back, shoulders, neck, arms and thighs, and some people have several of them.

Lipomas are almost always benign (not cancer). However, they can grow over time, press on nerves and cause discomfort, or simply be visible and bothersome. Any lump that grows quickly, feels hard or is painful should be examined by a surgeon to confirm the diagnosis.

Veinex Health offers quick, scar-conscious lipoma removal in Bangalore. Most lipomas are removed under local anaesthesia through a small incision placed along natural skin lines, so you go home the same day with a neat, minimal scar.

Signs

Signs of a lipoma

A soft, rubbery lump under the skin
Moves slightly when pressed
Grows slowly over months or years
Usually painless (can hurt if it presses on a nerve)
Usually less than 5 cm, but can grow larger
Can occur as one or several lumps

See a surgeon if a lump grows quickly, feels hard or fixed, is painful, or is larger than 5 cm. These features need investigation to rule out other conditions.

Details

What causes lipoma?

  • Family history (inherited tendency)
  • Most common between ages 40 and 60
  • Minor injury to the area (in some cases)
  • Certain genetic conditions (multiple lipomas)
  • Obesity may play a role
  • Often no clear cause

Lipoma removal options at Veinex Health, Bangalore

The technique depends on the size and location of the lipoma.

Surgical excision

The lipoma is removed completely with its capsule through a small incision, which minimises the chance of recurrence.

Minimal-incision (squeeze) technique

For small, soft lipomas, removal through a tiny incision for the smallest possible scar.

Liposuction-assisted removal

Large lipomas can be reduced with liposuction through tiny cuts, with less scarring.

Histopathology

The removed tissue can be sent for testing to confirm the diagnosis.
