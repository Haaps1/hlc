Overview

What are piles (hemorrhoids)?

Piles, also called hemorrhoids, are swollen blood vessels in and around the anus and lower rectum. Everyone has these vascular cushions; they become "piles" when they enlarge, bleed, itch or slip out of the anus. Piles are one of the most common reasons people in Bangalore visit a proctologist, and about half of all adults experience them at some point.

Long working hours at a desk, irregular meals, low water intake and a diet low in fibre make piles especially common among Bangalore's IT professionals, drivers and new mothers. Many people put off treatment out of embarrassment, but untreated piles usually get worse over time.

The good news: modern piles treatment in Bangalore no longer means painful open surgery. At Veinex Health we use laser hemorrhoidoplasty (LHP), a day-care procedure that shrinks piles from inside without cuts or stitches, so most patients return to desk work within 2–3 days.

Signs

Symptoms of piles

Bright red bleeding during or after passing stool
Itching or irritation around the anus
A soft lump or swelling near the anus
Pain or discomfort while sitting
Feeling of incomplete bowel emptying
Mucus discharge after passing stool

Don't self-diagnose rectal bleeding. It can also be caused by fissures, polyps or, rarely, colorectal cancer. A quick, private examination with our proctologist confirms the cause.

Causes

What causes piles (Hemorrhoids)?

  • Chronic constipation and straining
  • Low-fibre diet and low water intake
  • Sitting for long hours at a desk or while driving
  • Pregnancy and childbirth
  • Obesity and lack of exercise
  • Heavy lifting, chronic cough and ageing
Types

The 4 grades of internal piles

Doctors grade internal piles by how far they prolapse. Tap each grade to see what it means and how it's treated.

1

Grade 1

Piles stay inside the anal canal. You may notice painless bleeding while passing stool.

Treatment: Diet changes, medicines or a quick laser procedure.

2

Grade 2

Piles come out while passing stool but go back inside on their own. Itching and discomfort are common.

Treatment: Laser hemorrhoidoplasty is highly effective at this stage.

3

Grade 3

Piles come out and must be pushed back in by hand. Bleeding, pain and mucus discharge increase.

Treatment: Laser or stapler procedure is usually recommended.

4

Grade 4

Piles stay outside and cannot be pushed back. They can clot (thrombose) and become very painful.

Treatment: Needs surgical treatment soon. Laser is still an option in many cases.

Treatment

Piles treatment options at Veinex Health, Bangalore

Your proctologist recommends the right option after examining the grade and type of piles. Most patients are suitable for laser treatment.

Laser hemorrhoidoplasty (LHP)

A thin laser fibre shrinks the piles mass from within by sealing its blood supply. No cuts or stitches, minimal pain and same-day discharge.

Stapler hemorrhoidopexy (MIPH)

Used for large prolapsing piles. A circular stapler lifts the piles back into place and cuts off their blood supply.

Rubber band ligation

An OPD procedure for early internal piles. A tiny band cuts off blood flow, and the pile falls off within a week.

Medicines & lifestyle care

For early piles: stool softeners, fibre, sitz baths and ointments, with a diet plan from our team.
