Overview

What is appendicitis?

Appendicitis is inflammation of the appendix, a small finger-shaped pouch attached to the large intestine in the lower right abdomen. It usually happens when the appendix gets blocked by hardened stool, swelling or infection, and it is one of the most common causes of emergency abdominal surgery.

The classic sign is pain that starts around the belly button and moves to the lower right side of the abdomen over a few hours, getting worse with movement, coughing or walking. If it isn't treated, an inflamed appendix can burst (perforate) and spread infection through the abdomen (peritonitis), which is life-threatening.

Veinex Health provides 24×7 appendicitis surgery in Bangalore. Our surgeons confirm the diagnosis quickly with examination, blood tests and ultrasound or CT, then remove the appendix by laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery. That means less pain, tiny scars and a faster return home.

Signs

Symptoms of appendicitis

Pain in the lower right abdomen
Pain that starts near the navel and moves to the right
Pain that worsens with coughing, walking or bumps
Nausea and vomiting
Low-grade fever
Loss of appetite, constipation or diarrhoea

Appendicitis is a medical emergency. Don't take painkillers or laxatives to mask the pain. Get examined as soon as possible, especially if the pain is severe or you have a fever.

Details

What causes appendicitis?

  • Blockage of the appendix by hardened stool (faecolith)
  • Swelling of lymph tissue after infection
  • Gastrointestinal infections
  • Most common between ages 10 and 30
  • Can occur at any age
  • Family history may play a role
