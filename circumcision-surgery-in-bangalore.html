Overview

What is circumcision?

Circumcision is the surgical removal of the foreskin, the skin covering the head of the penis. It's done for medical reasons, such as a tight foreskin (phimosis), a foreskin stuck behind the head (paraphimosis) or repeated infections (balanitis and balanoposthitis), as well as for religious, cultural or personal hygiene reasons.

Many men delay treatment for years because of embarrassment, while living with pain, infections or difficulty in intimacy. Modern techniques have made circumcision quick, safe and far more comfortable than before.

Veinex Health offers confidential circumcision surgery in Bangalore using stapler and laser techniques. Stapler circumcision gives a precise, even cut with minimal bleeding and no stitches to remove, so healing is faster and the cosmetic result is neat. The procedure takes 15–30 minutes and you go home the same day.

Common reasons for circumcision

Tight foreskin that won't pull back (phimosis)
Repeated infection or inflammation (balanitis)
Foreskin stuck behind the head (paraphimosis)
Pain or tearing during intercourse
Ballooning of the foreskin or difficulty passing urine
Religious, cultural or personal hygiene reasons

Paraphimosis, where the foreskin is stuck behind the head and swollen, is an emergency. Seek care immediately to restore blood flow.

Circumcision techniques at Veinex Health, Bangalore

Your surgeon recommends the technique based on your age, anatomy and preference.

Stapler circumcision

A disposable device removes the foreskin and seals the edge with dissolvable staples: precise, fast and virtually bloodless.

Laser circumcision

Laser cuts and seals tissue at the same time, reducing bleeding and swelling.

Conventional circumcision

The traditional surgical technique with fine dissolvable stitches, suitable in selected cases.

Paediatric circumcision

Safe, gentle circumcision for children with phimosis or recurrent infections.
