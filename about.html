Who We Are

A day-care surgical centre built around you

Veinex Health is a specialist clinic on 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, focused on minimally invasive treatment in four areas: proctology, vascular surgery, laparoscopy and general surgery. We're best known for varicose veins and piles treatment in Bangalore.

Our surgeons have performed more than 25,000 procedures. Patient coordinators guide you through every step, from the first consultation and insurance approval to recovery and follow-up, so you always know what's next.

  • USFDA-approved lasers
  • Modular operation theatres
  • Colour Doppler & diagnostics
  • Cashless insurance
  • Free pick-up & drop
  • Transparent pricing

What We Treat

Our specialities in Bangalore

Proctology

Laser treatment for piles, fissure, fistula and pilonidal sinus: painless, private, day-care procedures by experienced proctologists.

  • Piles (Hemorrhoids) treatment
  • Anal Fissure treatment
  • Fistula-in-Ano treatment
  • Pilonidal Sinus treatment

Vascular Surgery

Vein and circulation care, from endovenous laser for varicose veins to limb-saving treatment for diabetic foot ulcers.

  • Varicose Veins treatment
  • Diabetic Foot treatment

Laparoscopy

Keyhole surgery for hernia and gallbladder stones: tiny scars, less pain and a hospital stay of about 24 hours.

  • Hernia treatment
  • Gallbladder Stones treatment

General Surgery

Day-care procedures for liposuction, appendicitis, hydrocele, lipoma and circumcision, with confidential, patient-first care.

  • Liposuction treatment
  • Appendicitis treatment
  • Hydrocele treatment
  • Lipoma treatment
  • Circumcision treatment
