Who We Are

A day-care surgical centre built around you

HLC is a specialist clinic in Indiranagar, Bangalore, focused on minimally invasive laser and laparoscopic treatment for proctology, general surgery, vascular and cosmetic conditions.

Our surgeons have performed more than 25,000 procedures. Our patient coordinators guide you through every step, from the first consultation and insurance approval to recovery and follow-up.

  • USFDA-approved lasers
  • Modular operation theatres
  • Dedicated patient coordinator
  • Cashless insurance
  • Free pick-up & drop
  • Transparent pricing
