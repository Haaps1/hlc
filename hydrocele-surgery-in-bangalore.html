Overview

What is a hydrocele?

A hydrocele is a collection of fluid in the thin sac around a testicle, which causes the scrotum to swell. It's usually painless, but as it grows it can feel heavy, uncomfortable and embarrassing, and it can make walking, sitting or exercise difficult.

Hydroceles are common in newborns and often resolve on their own. In adult men they may develop because of inflammation, injury or infection, or with no clear cause. In some parts of India, filarial infection can also cause a hydrocele. A hydrocele that is large, growing or bothersome in an adult usually needs surgery.

Veinex Health offers safe, confidential hydrocele surgery in Bangalore. The standard procedure, hydrocelectomy, removes or folds the sac so fluid can't collect again. It's a short day-care procedure with a very low chance of recurrence. We also examine you carefully to rule out other causes of scrotal swelling, such as a hernia.

Signs

Symptoms of hydrocele

Painless swelling of one or both sides of the scrotum
A feeling of heaviness in the scrotum
Swelling that slowly increases over time
Discomfort while walking or sitting
Swelling that may be bigger in the evening
Difficulty with clothing or exercise

Sudden, severe scrotal pain is an emergency. It may be testicular torsion, which needs surgery within hours. Seek care immediately.

Details

What causes hydrocele?

  • Fluid imbalance in the sac around the testicle
  • Inflammation or infection (epididymitis)
  • Injury to the scrotum
  • Filarial infection (in some regions)
  • Present from birth (congenital)
  • After hernia or other groin surgery
Treatment

Hydrocele treatment at Veinex Health, Bangalore

Surgery is the most reliable treatment for a hydrocele in adults.

Hydrocelectomy

The fluid is drained and the sac removed or everted (Jaboulay's procedure) so fluid can't collect again.

Plication (Lord's procedure)

The sac is folded with fine stitches. Less tissue handling, suited to thin-walled hydroceles.

Paediatric hydrocele repair

For children whose hydrocele doesn't resolve by 1–2 years or is linked to a hernia.

Ultrasound evaluation

A scrotal ultrasound confirms the diagnosis and checks the testicle before surgery.

Hydrocele Surgery in Bangalore: FAQs

For adults with a large or bothersome hydrocele, hydrocelectomy is the most reliable treatment. It's a short day-care surgery with a low chance of recurrence.

In infants, hydroceles often disappear on their own. In adults, medicines don't remove the fluid, and draining it with a needle usually leads to it coming back. Surgery is the permanent solution.

The surgery is done under anaesthesia. Afterwards there's mild swelling and discomfort for a few days, managed with medicines and scrotal support.

It depends on the size of the hydrocele, whether one or both sides are affected and the anaesthesia. You'll get a clear estimate at consultation, and hydrocele surgery is covered by most insurance policies.

A simple hydrocele usually doesn't affect fertility. A very large, long-standing one may raise scrotal temperature, and an ultrasound helps rule out other problems.
