Overview

What are gallbladder stones?

Gallbladder stones (gallstones) are hard deposits that form in the gallbladder, a small pouch under the liver that stores bile for digesting fat. They form when bile contains too much cholesterol or bilirubin. Some people have one large stone, others have hundreds of tiny ones.

Many gallstones cause no symptoms and are found on a routine ultrasound. But when a stone blocks the gallbladder outlet, it causes biliary colic: intense pain in the upper right or middle abdomen, often after a heavy or oily meal. Stones can also lead to gallbladder infection (cholecystitis), jaundice and pancreatitis, which can be serious.

The standard, proven gallbladder stone treatment in Bangalore and worldwide is laparoscopic cholecystectomy: removing the gallbladder through keyhole surgery. Your body digests food normally without a gallbladder, because the liver keeps making bile. At Veinex Health, most patients go home within 24 hours.

Signs

Symptoms of gallbladder stones

Pain in the upper right or upper middle abdomen
Pain after fatty or heavy meals, often at night
Pain spreading to the back or right shoulder
Nausea and vomiting
Bloating, gas and indigestion
Yellow eyes or skin, dark urine, fever (complications)

Fever with abdominal pain, yellow eyes or skin, or severe pain lasting several hours needs urgent care. These can signal infection, a blocked bile duct or pancreatitis.

Causes

What causes gallbladder Stones?

  • Excess cholesterol in bile
  • Diet high in fat and refined carbohydrates
  • Obesity or rapid weight loss
  • Being female, pregnancy and hormone therapy
  • Diabetes
  • Family history and age over 40

Reduced-port surgery

ERCP (for bile duct stones)
